# Five-Minute Demo Video Plan

## 0:00-0:35 - Problem and user

Show the farmer's problem quickly: fragmented scheme discovery, complex conditions, and language/access friction.

## 0:35-0:55 - Baseline

Run the same farmer case through the simple baseline. Highlight one concrete limitation such as guessed eligibility, missed missing information, or unsupported detail.

## 0:55-3:20 - One realistic KisanPath journey

Recommended demo:

1. farmer speaks Gujarati;
2. transcript appears;
3. profile facts are extracted;
4. one critical value is confirmed or one missing question is asked;
5. relevant schemes are discovered;
6. eligibility conditions are shown;
7. evidence is verified;
8. final Gujarati explanation is displayed and spoken.

Do not spend most of the video on architecture slides.

## 3:20-4:00 - Measured comparison

Show the primary metric and a few supporting metrics for baseline vs final on the frozen cases.

## 4:00-4:35 - Improvement changelog

Show 3-5 meaningful iterations and identify which contributed most.

## 4:35-5:00 - Removed experiment and hot take

Show one experiment that did not help and the practical lesson learned. A strong candidate insight is that explicit deterministic rules and evidence verification improved reliability more than simply adding more autonomous agents.
